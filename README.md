## A little boost for student life

UniBoost was an iOS app I helped build around a simple idea: **someone on your campus already knows what you're trying to learn.** It brought course discussions, study resources, and student connections into one place, making it easier to ask for help and share something useful in return.

| Ask & discuss | Share & discover | Connect & organize |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ask course questions and learn from students who have taken the class before. | Share notes and study guides, and find textbooks from other students. | Discover classmates and tutors, build a profile, and organize your course timetable. |

> **Project status:** No longer maintained or available on the App Store. This repository preserves the original iOS source and visual assets as a portfolio project.
